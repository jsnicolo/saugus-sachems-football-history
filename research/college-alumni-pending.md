# Saugus football alumni — research leads and evidence standards

Updated 2026-11-01. This is an **incomplete working inventory**, not the published alumni list. The searchable website section reads `college-alumni.json`.

## Standard

Include a Saugus **High School football** alumnus on a verified college or professional football roster, whether or not they played. Freshmen, reserves, redshirts, and professional practice-squad members count. A scholarship offer, draft pick, tryout, hometown of Saugus, or attending a college for a different sport is **not sufficient** by itself. Confirm that the player attended Saugus High School in **Massachusetts**, not the school in Santa Clarita, California. Do not infer high school graduation years from college class standing without labeling them as estimates.

The August 18, 1967 Globe Agganis preview identifies Ed Maguire of Saugus with Indiana; the school Hall of Fame roster identifies Edward J. MaGuire, SHS Class of 1967.
