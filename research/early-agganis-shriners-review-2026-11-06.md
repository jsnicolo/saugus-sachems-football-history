## Result

Added Joe Attubato, 1994, to Shriners high-school football honors. The existing linked 1994 Lynn Item preview explicitly discusses his participation in the Shriners game. Its preceding page names the departing player. Source: [continuation](../documents/1994-item-attubato-agganis-captain.webp), [context](../documents/1994-item-attubato-agganis-context.webp).

The Agganis Football Classic started in 1956. The foundation executive director dates its start in this [June 25, 2013 Lynn Journal interview](https://lynnjournal.com/2013/06/25/agganis-classics-week-soars-to-new-heights/). The [City of Lynn stadium history](https://www.lynncommunitydevelopment.com/manningandfrasierfields) records games at Manning Bowl in 1956–1959 and again from 1965. The contributed program history also describes the 1965 resumption.

No pre-1976 Saugus Agganis game selection was verified in this pass. This is an unresolved research gap, not a finding that Saugus had no participants.

## Newly supplied contemporary sources · November 6, 2026

The earlier pre-1976 gap is now partially resolved by two user-supplied Globe clippings, retained as source documents only, with no gallery entries.

- 1956: Dick Barrows, Saugus, Essex backfield. Boston Daily Globe, December 1, 1956, Sports page 5, “12,000 Expected At Agganis Fund Game Tomorrow.” First annual game, December 2, 1956, Manning Bowl, 1:45 p.m. Saugus was represented in the inaugural game.
- 1957: Dick Rohrbacher, Saugus, right guard, North Shore probable starting lineup. Boston Globe, December 8, 1957, page 51, “All-Star Starting Lineups.” Game that day at Manning Bowl, 1:30 p.m.

These early editions were played in December; summer-only archive searches would miss them. Graduation classes remain unassigned without separate evidence. Future supplied images default to sources only unless the user requests gallery inclusion.

## 1958 selections supplied November 6, 2026

- Mike Light, end, and John Naples, guard: listed as Light and Naples in the Lynn–Swampscott roster in the Globe report of the December 7 third annual Agganis game. Full names and Saugus affiliation identified by Sal Nicolo. Lynn–Swampscott won 14–6 over Salem–Beverly at Manning Bowl.
- Boston Globe, Monday, December 1, 1958, “2 All-Star School Games Sunday,” explicitly explains that the Greater Lynn side included Saugus and other communities, resolving the roster’s regional team label. Both clippings are source documents only. Graduation classes not inferred.
