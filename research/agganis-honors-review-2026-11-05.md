# Agganis football honors review — November 5, 2026

Added 20 missing game selections, bringing the honors section from 8 to 28. Standardized the selecting organization as Harry Agganis All-Star game. Game years are separate from the preceding fall football season.

## Sources reviewed

- Football.zip and Football(2).zip have identical SHA-256 hashes. Reviewed the unique archive once; also reviewed Sachem All Stars.zip.
- Screened 784 submitted standalone image files, deduplicating exact byte matches; searched OCR from the archive, submitted sources and current gallery. Visually checked relevant game history pages, the Attubato article, Mabee illustration and Spadafora feature. OCR screening cannot establish that every faint or damaged image has yielded every possible name.
- Checked both supplied season-record workbooks for Agganis mentions. Source-only selections retain explicit workbook attribution; game years for those records are inferred from the preceding fall season and labeled accordingly.
- Excluded weekly Item/Agganis awards, college Agganis awards, scholarship-only mentions, other sports, and references to Harry Agganis as an opposing player.

## Corrections and evidence

- Mark Spadafora: retained 2004 game year, added Class of 2004 and the Globe feature as evidence. Corrected its gallery year from 2005 to 2004. The feature says he graduated that spring and was one of 16 scholarship recipients. The Agganis Foundation scholarship register places him in 2004: https://www.agganisfoundation.com/_files/ugd/855faa_56432990001a47fcbfa718c3548daf9c.pdf .
- Program game history supports John Coffey (1978), Paul Nigro (1979), Steve Peach (1981), Joe Barressi (1988), Chris Mikulski (1991), L.J. McKanas (1997), and Raphael Zammit (1999).
- Program records Joe Barressi as East co-MVP, Marc Fauci as East MVP, Chris Mikulski as East MVP, and Raphael Zammit as South MVP.
- The two-page 1994 Lynn Item preview names Joe Attubato and calls him a captain in the Agganis game. The Hank Collins illustration identifies Mike Mabee as an Agganis football and baseball selection.
- Source images added as linked documents only. No gallery additions were made for this review.
