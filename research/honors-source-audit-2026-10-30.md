# Saugus High football honors — source audit (October 30, 2026)

## Status

The honors database now contains **177 award records**, up from **134** before this research pass. These are *award records*, not unique players. The 43 new entries consist of nine 1941 Boston Globe honorable mentions, eight 1944 North Shore All-Star game selections, seven 1969 NEC honors, eighteen additional NEC awards spanning 1994–2015, and Sal Nicolo's 2003 Harry Agganis football All-Star selection. All new records have source links and an explicit distinction. The existing 134 records were retained, with selected name and award-level corrections. The index now displays a clickable evidence column.

## Primary evidence consulted

- **1941 Boston Globe All-Scholastic** original scan: `photos/contributed-2690.webp`. Mike Harrington is **second team**, not first. The scan lists nine Saugus **honorable mentions**: ends Collette and Goclowski; tackles MacDonald and Pendlebury; guards Daniels and Flaherty; center Art Statuto; backs Eddie Shipulski and Goss. Surname-only entries need first-name identification.
- **1944 Boston Globe** original scan: `photos/IMG_3718.webp`. Art Spinney is first-team end; Frank Pyzsko is second-team back.
- **1945 Tontoquonian**, 1944 season post-season review: https://archive.org/details/tontoquonian1945saug . Original supplied ZIP scan `IMG_2767.jpg` names **eight** Saugus selections to the North Shore All-Star game: Art Spinney (captain), Dick Allen, Don Reiniger, John Quinlan, Bob Thurell, Bruce Waybright, Frank Pyzsko and **Bob Kane**, who was unable to play because of an ankle injury. This is a **game selection**, not a newspaper All-Scholastic or All-State honor.
- **1969 football yearbook review**, `photos/season1969-2796.webp`: NEC first offense Bob Osgood and Bill Eichel; first defense Steve Nelson; honorable mention Rich DeSisto, Bill Babcock, Rich Conti and John Richard. Do not conflate NEC honorable mention with first-team selection.
- **1994–2015 Item individual capsules**: sources linked on each newly added NEC row. 1996 L.J. McKanas earned **NEC Offensive Player of the Year**, which is separately labeled and does not by itself establish an All-State selection.
- **2000 and 2001 Tontoquonian**: https://archive.org/details/tontoquonian2000saug and https://archive.org/details/tontoquonian2001saug . The yearbook texts identify George Lowe as a 1999 NEC All-Star and Ryan Gould and Billy Robinson as 2000 All-Conference players. Item's 2000 Ryan Gould capsule independently confirms his NEC status.
- **2003 Item**, `photos/honors-IMG_3760.webp`: Anthony Barbanti was a **three-time** NEC All-Star and two-time Item All-Star. His **2002 and 2003** NEC seasons have direct year-specific evidence. Do **not** automatically create a 2001 NEC entry without season confirmation.
- **2003 Harry Agganis All-Star**, `photos/contributed-IMG_2477.webp`: Sal Nicolo identified in the original gallery clipping.

## Corrections and cautions

1. The 1945 Tontoquonian's fall **1944** schedule and its explicit 1944 Class B championship description support the 1944 season assignment. A 2023 retrospective column shifts some seasons by one year and names **Bob Evans** among the eight North Shore game players; the original yearbook explicitly names **Bob Kane** as chosen but unable to play. Retain Kane as the primary-source version, and keep the discrepancy open.
2. The 1958 Item selection names **Mike Light**, not Matt Light; 1957 names **Dick Rohrbacher**, not Rohrbacker; the 1966 source names **Edward Maguire**, not McGuire; 1994–95 names **Carlo Abate**, not Carlos; the 2014 Item names **Dan Cacciola**, not Cicciola.
3. 2022 Item player caption identifies **Braden Faiella as a junior**, while the original workbook called him a senior in both 2022 and 2023. The 2022 honors record now shows junior and 2023 senior. Asterisks from the original workbook remain pending interpretation.
4. The 2023 Boston Globe Art Spinney entry is an **all-time retrospective selection**, not a 2023 playing-season award.
5. The 1958 Boston Globe William Banks selection is a **reserve**, not a first-team All-Scholastic. Similar first/second/reserve distinctions need verification for other early Globe entries.
6. The 1990 team yearbook `IMG_2787.jpg` reports **seven NEC All-Stars**, but the passage does **not** identify all seven. Do not fabricate seven named selections.
7. Some source images are not yet on the public gallery. The 390-image ZIP's OCR index is an aid, not proof that every original image was fully reviewed. Visual verification and additional scans remain necessary.

- Review newer 2025 and eventual 2026 selections when published; do not invent 2026 honors before selection announcements.
