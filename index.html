06 / The research desk

An archive built to grow.

This archive brings together season records, photographs, player histories, and research supplied by Sal Nicolo. “Source supplied” means a claim appears in these documents; it does not mean it has been independently verified.

Research notes & items to reconcile
  • The reported 1966, 1969 and 1987 titles remain unverified. The 1922 championship has a historical source qualification. The 1959 championship team is recognized by the school district, but the exact title designation is unresolved. See the sourced championship audit above.
  • The 1922 championship claim is disputed in the narrative; the 9–0–2 season is recorded separately from that claim.
  • Dave Lucey’s spelling and Dean Sacca’s 1999–2000 tenure were confirmed by Sal Nicolo. John / Jon Janusas still varies between sources.
  • The Dean Sacca narrative’s misplaced 2001 references have been corrected to 2000. Original downloads retain their supplied wording.
  • The season table retains the narrative’s 2025 record (2–9); the newer honors, alumni & stats workbook lists 3–8 for 2025. This difference needs reconciliation.
  • The 1918 entry has no record. The narrative says no season was found; the reason is not established.
  • Unusual season totals, including 2015 and 2022, are reproduced as supplied and need schedule-level confirmation.

Future additions: newspaper citations, game-by-game results, team photos, verified inductee lists, and additional individual statistical records. This archive is independent and is not an official school publication.
