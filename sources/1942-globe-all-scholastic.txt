1942 Boston Globe All-Scholastic selections — Saugus

Award evidence: contributor-supplied image F2DBD778-F87F-4AFB-8CEF-18D81D7D66BA.png, provided by Sal Nicolo November 7, 2026.
Printed heading: Globe ’42 All-Scholastic Eleven.
Exact issue date and page are outside the crop.

Leonard “Lennie” Flaherty — Guard — All-Scholastic third team. Globe name: Len Flaherty.
Nicholas “Nick” Barressi — End — All-Scholastic honorable mention. Globe name: Baresi.
Robert “Bob” Connell — Tackle — All-Scholastic honorable mention. Globe name: Connell.
Harold “Doo” Pendlebury — Tackle — All-Scholastic honorable mention. Globe name: Pendlebury.
Donald “Don” Grimes — Guard — All-Scholastic honorable mention. Globe name: Grimes.
Arthur “Art” Statuto — Center — All-Scholastic honorable mention. Globe name: Statuto.
Edward “Eddie” Godett — Back — All-Scholastic honorable mention. Globe name: Godett.
Russell “Russ” Babcock — Back — All-Scholastic honorable mention. Globe name: Babcock.

Identity evidence: 1943 Tontoquonian senior biographies explicitly identify all eight names, nicknames and football participation. The football team photograph roster and season review place the players on the fall 1942 team. The yearbook explicitly identifies HAROLD PENDLEBURY, nickname Doo; this supports Harold for the 1942 selection despite a conflicting Robert expansion in an older archive entry.
