Saugus entries:

These are honorable mentions, not first- or second-team selections.

Identity cross-check: 1938 Tontoquonian, Saugus High School yearbook.

The senior biographies identify Russell Collamore (football; nickname Russ), George McKay (football, baseball, basketball; explicitly nicknamed Bud), and Wendell Brooks (football, baseball, basketball). The football review explicitly covers fall 1937 and marks Collamore, McKay and Brooks as seniors.
Use Russell “Russ” Collamore, George “Bud” McKay and Wendell Brooks; Class of 1938. The Globe spells Collamore as Collomore; the yearbook supplies the preferred full name.
