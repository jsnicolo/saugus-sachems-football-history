Full-name evidence:
Robert “Bob” Burns — 1939 Tontoquonian senior biography, ROBERT BURNS, lists four years of football. Class history explicitly calls him Bob Burns and identifies him among the senior football players.
https://archive.org/details/tontoquonian1939saug

Frank DeMaso — 1940 Tontoquonian senior biography, FRANK DEMASO, lists football and baseball; class history also names Frank Demaso as a football player. Yearbook typography varies Demaso/DeMaso; the Globe prints De Maso.
https://archive.org/details/tontoquonian1940saug
https://archive.org/download/tontoquonian1940saug/tontoquonian1940saug_djvu.txt

The 1938 squad photograph and season review independently place Burns and Demaso on the same team, and describe both as scorers.
