Sardi Nicolo — 1949 North Shore scoring feature

Primary source: “Thrill of Scoring Spurs Sardi Nicolo to Heights,” original IMG_3026.jpg in the contributor-supplied Football(2).zip collection. The clipping was visually checked for the November 8, 2026 biography review. Its newspaper masthead and exact date are not visible.

The feature reports that Sardi scored three touchdowns against Chelsea to move into the North Shore scoring lead. At that point he had nine touchdowns and 54 points through five games, ahead of Billy Ransom of Beverly and Jack Lucido of Gloucester, each with 42 points.

It identifies Sardi as an 18-year-old senior in his third varsity football season and describes the blocking of guard Danny Murphy and brother Richie Nicolo. This is a midseason report, not his final 1949 scoring total. The separate season wrap-up credits him with 16 touchdowns, three extra points and 99 points.
