Senior biographies identify RICHARD ALLEN and DONALD REINIGER with football participation. The fall 1944 team roster lists R. Allen, D. Reiniger and R. Kane. Its post-season account identifies Dick Allen, Don Reiniger and Bob Kane; season highlights describe Bob Kane’s running and tackling against Melrose.
Bob is the verified given-name form for Kane in this yearbook; a longer form is not established by the reviewed evidence and is not inferred.

The honorable mentions are separate from their existing North Shore All-Star game honors. No duplicate Spinney or Pyzsko Globe selections created. Image retained as source-only, not added to gallery.
