1950 North Shore vs. Suburban football All-Star game — Saugus selections

Source: Boston Globe, Gene Mack Jr., report headed “Waltham Star Scores All the Points Again as North Shore Eleven Bows,” datelined Salem, Dec. 3. Season identified by contributor as 1950. The game was played December 3, 1950 at Bertram Field, Salem. Suburban won 19–12.
Contributor-supplied full article IMG_3253.jpeg and enlarged roster 2F20D84C-A26F-4A78-991B-6CA10F289B9E.png, supplied by Sal Nicolo November 7, 2026.

North Shore roster, Saugus entries:
Right end — Woodell (Saugus): Charles “Charlie” Woodell.
Right tackle — Kane (Saugus): Leo Kane.
Quarterback — Nicolo (Saugus): Richard “Richie” Nicolo.
Right halfback — Driscoll (Saugus): James “Jim” Driscoll.

The article explicitly says Richie Nicolo sat on the bench nursing a knee injury suffered in Lynn Saturday; retain his All-Star selection but do not claim he played. The other three players appear on the game roster; no individual playing-time claim is inferred.

Full-name evidence: 1951 Tontoquonian senior biographies identify Charles Woodell, Leo Kane, Richard Nicolo and James Driscoll; its 1950 football review uses Charlie Woodell, Richie Nicolo and Jim Driscoll.
